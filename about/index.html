A little about me.

An analog engineer with a software habit and a curiosity that doesn’t switch off.

Dinesh, or just Dino.

Senior Analog Design Engineer
NVIDIA

Say hello

I design analog and mixed-signal ICs, currently at NVIDIA. My current focus is precision voltage and current references, comparators, and supply-monitoring circuits.

Earlier roles at Microsoft, Intel, and Texas Instruments took me through on-chip power sensing, high-speed receivers, switching power conversion, and clock generation. I studied electrical engineering at IIT Madras and Texas A&M.

References, receivers, and control loops.

The design problem changes with the circuit. References call for attention to noise, startup, and drift; receivers to input range, headroom, and delay; and power and clocking loops to stability and transient behavior. My work connects those circuit-level choices with simulation, layout parasitics, and chip integration.

Beyond the schematic.

I build things at home that I should probably just buy. Right now that means LLM bots, a homelab that serves my family, and a small robot that talks back.

My personal projects explore tools for learning and finding information, home automation, self-hosted services, and robotics. They give me room to experiment with software and follow my curiosity outside work.
